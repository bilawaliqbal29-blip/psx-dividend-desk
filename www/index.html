Import market data

Update market data

Paste tables from the PSX data portal below. The app reads either table and combines them.

  1. Every week, before you buy: open the PSX screener, press Search with no filters, copy the whole results table from the header row down, paste it here and press Import. This brings in prices, dividend yields and P/E ratios.
  2. Once a month: do the same with Market Watch. This adds each company's sector and its "Listed in" indices. The Shariah filter and the one-sector limit need it.

Copy on a computer if you can. Chrome and Edge keep the columns separate, which Market Watch needs because sector names contain spaces.

Files work too: a CSV saved from the screener, or the daily closing-rates file from PSX Downloads (the .Z file), which updates prices only.

Dividend history for one company

Optional, but it sharpens the steadiness score. Open a company page such as OGDC, go to Payouts, copy the table and paste it here.

Settings

Trading costs cover broker commission, CDC and exchange charges, and tax on commission. Check your broker's rate card. For small orders, 0.2–0.3% is typical.

Your data

Data is saved on this device. When you're signed in, it also syncs to your account so you can use it on your phone and computer.

This tool ranks shares with a fixed formula on data you import. It is not investment advice. Check a company's latest results and announcements before you buy.
